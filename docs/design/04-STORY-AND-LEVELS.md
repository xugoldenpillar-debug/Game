# Story and Levels v0.1

## World premise

Near-future urban setting. A major technology corporation, **NOVA Dynamics**, develops an experimental enhancement program known as **Project RED**. A containment failure releases unstable test subjects and triggers a corporate/military lockdown.

The player character, temporarily known as **Zero**, appears to be an ordinary civilian caught in the evacuation. Over time, it becomes clear that Zero was previously the most successful Project RED subject and had their memories intentionally erased.

## Campaign target

- 8 major chapters
- 40-50 main missions
- 20+ side missions
- campaign target: 8-12 hours
- completionist target: 15-25 hours

## Chapter 1: City Collapse

Themes:
- streets
- convenience store
- parking structures
- subway entrance
- police/military barricades

Goals:
- teach movement
- teach melee
- introduce pickups
- introduce pistol/firearm basics
- introduce throwables

Boss: enraged escaped test subject.

## Chapter 7: Project RED

Zero regains critical memories and discovers the designation **RED-00**.

Late-game abilities may include:
- temporary strength boost
- brief slow-motion state
- accelerated recovery
- impact strike
- enhanced mobility

These should not invalidate the grounded combat systems built earlier.

## Chapter 8: NOVA Tower

Final assault through:
- lobby
- offices
- research sectors
- security center
- server levels
- executive floors
- rooftop

Final boss should have multiple phases and reveal the final truth behind Project RED.

The ending may branch based on mission choices and companion outcomes.

## Mission structure guideline

Avoid a constant "walk right and kill everything" format.

A typical mission can include:

entry -> combat -> exploration branch -> resource/secret -> story event -> large encounter -> traversal -> elite fight -> checkpoint -> boss/set piece.

Suggested standard mission length: 10-25 minutes.

Large story mission length: up to 30-40 minutes.

Unlocked mid-campaign. Potential functions:
- loadout management
- skill allocation
- companion selection
- side mission access
- story conversations
